Claude reads your CV, works out your real experience level and picks the job titles you'd get shortlisted for.

Where the jobs come from

Live jobs are pulled straight from the hiring systems of over 80 companies with teams in India (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and SmartRecruiters), including Databricks, Stripe, MongoDB, Okta, Zscaler, Sarvam AI, Meesho, Paytm, Swiggy and Bosch. Each title opens that exact job on the company's own careers page, where you apply directly.

Only titles that match your target roles and level are shown. Senior, lead, manager and non-engineering roles are left out.

LinkedIn and Naukri tabs open filtered searches on those sites, sorted newest first. No Google, Indeed or job-copying sites anywhere.

Before you apply

  • On LinkedIn and Naukri, skip posts from consultancies that won't name the client, and anything marked "Reposted".
  • On LinkedIn, skip posts from job-relay accounts (names like "Jobs via…", Jobgether, Talent.com). They are middlemen posting other companies' jobs.
  • If the post links to the company's careers page, apply there rather than through the board.
  • Never pay a fee. Real employers don't charge for interviews, training or offer letters.

Newest first

Live jobs are sorted by the date the company published them, and the date filter starts at the past month (switch to 24 hours or 3 days for only the freshest). Applying in the first day or two gets you seen before the recruiter's inbox fills up. Use Mark applied to keep track.

Links you've opened turn purple, so you can see what you've already checked.
